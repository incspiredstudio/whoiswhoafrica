# Visitor flows

This section describes what a visitor can do on the public site and what happens behind each action. Nobody needs an account for any of it. Where a flow ends with staff work, the page links to the matching part of the staff guide.

## Flows at a glance

| Flow | What the visitor does | Staff side |
|---|---|---|
| [Directory and profiles](/visitor/directory-and-profiles) | Browse, filter and read profiles | [Profile review](/staff/profile-review) |
| [Search](/visitor/search) | Find a profile or story by name or keyword | Automatic |
| [Stories](/visitor/stories) | Read stories, sign up for the newsletter, comment | [Stories](/staff/stories), [Newsletter](/staff/newsletter) |
| [Events](/visitor/events) | See what is coming up | [Events](/staff/events) |
| [Get listed](/visitor/get-listed) | Send an enquiry to the team | [Enquiries](/staff/enquiries) |
| [Claim a profile](/visitor/claim-a-profile) | Ask to take care of a profile | [Claims](/staff/claims) |
| [Corrections and removal requests](/visitor/corrections-and-removals) | Fix a fact, report a problem or ask for removal | [Corrections](/staff/corrections), [Requests and reports](/staff/requests-and-reports) |
| [Nominations](/visitor/nominations) | Nominate someone for an award | [Awards](/staff/awards) |

## What is common to every form

1. Forms ask only for what staff need. Personal contact details a visitor gives are never shown publicly.
2. Each form has a bot check and a limit on how often one device or address can send it.
3. After sending, the visitor sees a confirmation with a reference and receives an email with the same reference.
4. Where staff must be sure the sender owns the email address, the email contains a single-use link. See [The no-accounts model](/concepts/no-accounts).
5. Nothing changes publicly until a staff member acts.

## Time limits the site promises

| Promise | Time |
|---|---|
| Acknowledge a correction, removal request, report or complaint | 1 business day |
| Resolve a correction | 7 days |
| Hide a profile after a valid removal request from the person or their representative | 2 business days |
| Decide a claim | 5 days |

The FAQ states these promises. If a time limit ever changes, change the FAQ in the same step.
