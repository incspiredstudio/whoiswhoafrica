# Nominations

The awards recognise people and organisations, chosen by independent judges against criteria that are published before nominations open. The public site shows the rules, takes nominations while they are open, then publishes the nominees and the winners. Judging happens outside the site, and payment never affects who is nominated or who wins.

## What the public sees

| Page | Content |
|---|---|
| Awards overview | The edition, where the awards stand, the categories in five groups, how judging works and any supporters |
| Rules | Eligibility, nominations, judging and "No public voting" |
| Category page | The group, the winner if published, the nominees and how the category is judged |
| Nominees | Every live nominee, grouped by award group |
| Nominee page | Portrait, category, citation and an "On the record" extract from the reviewed profile. The nominator is never named. |
| Winners | Winners of every edition, newest first, with search and filters. A short confetti animation plays once, and is skipped for visitors who ask for reduced motion. |

Publicly there are only nominees and winners. A nominee page appears only when staff set the nomination Live.

## Nominate someone

Nominations are open only while an Administrator has the Nominations switch on. When it is off, the Nominate page says "Nominations are closed" and links to the nominees or winners if they are published, the rules and the overview. The same guidance (how it works, who can be nominated, criteria, categories, what to have ready, short answers) appears either way.

1. **Your details.** Name, email, relationship to the nominee and the privacy tick.
2. **Verify your email.** Open the single-use link sent to you. No account is created.
3. **Nominee.** Name, whether a person, business or institution, and an optional nominee email. A duplicate warning appears if the name is already nominated or suppressed. The visitor can continue.
4. **Category and evidence.** Choose a category, write a short evidence statement and add an optional link. Text containing contact details or sensitive words is blocked with a message.
5. **Conflict declaration.** No conflict, or a description of the relationship.
6. **Review and submit.** Check the summary, pass the bot check and send. The visitor gets a reference and "Nomination submitted".

A visitor who sends too many nominations from one device sees "Too many nominations from this device". There is no public voting.

## What happens to a nomination

1. It arrives as Nominated and Not live. Nobody can see it.
2. Staff link it to a reviewed profile and switch it to Live. It then appears on the Nominees page.
3. When the offline judging has decided, staff switch the stage to Winner. A Live winner appears on the Winners page.
4. The site does not email the nominee or ask them to accept. A nominee who objects uses "Request removal" on their profile. That hides the nomination and adds the name to the permanent suppression list.
