# Corrections and removal requests

Anyone can ask for a profile to be corrected, report a problem with it, or ask for it to be removed, without an account. This page covers the three profile actions and the data-rights form, and the time limits the site promises.

## Suggest a correction

1. Open the profile and choose "Suggest a correction".
2. Say who you are and what you need: correct a fact, add a right-of-reply note, change which contact details are public, switch enquiries on or off, or ask for the view count and sources.
3. Say which part of the profile, give the new value, add evidence and your email address.
4. If you say you are the person or their representative, open the single-use link sent to your email. The request reaches staff only after you click it.
5. Staff apply the change, mark it resolved or decline it with a reason. You are emailed when it is resolved.

Nothing changes publicly until staff act.

## Request removal

1. Open the profile and choose "Request removal".
2. Say who you are, why, and give your email address.
3. Staff check the request. If it is valid, the profile is hidden within two business days and the name goes on a permanent suppression list so it cannot be republished.
4. You are emailed the outcome.

A removed profile returns a "gone" page, leaves search and the sitemap, and its cached copy is cleared.

## Report this profile

1. Choose "Report" on the profile.
2. Pick a reason (inaccurate, defamatory or harmful, privacy, copyright, other) and add details.
3. Staff can hide the profile at once while they look into it, then resolve or dismiss the report.

## Your data rights

The data-rights page takes requests to access, correct, erase, object to the use of, or receive a copy of your data.

1. Open Your data rights from the footer.
2. Choose the request type, enter your name and email, and add details.
3. Complete the bot check and send it.
4. You receive a reference and an email. The request is tracked with a due date.

For a public profile, the fastest route is the form on that profile.

## Complaints and takedown

The complaints page covers defamation, privacy, copyright and accuracy. It tells the visitor to use the three actions above, says disputes go to an Editor, and repeats the commitments below.

## Time limits

| Step | Time |
|---|---|
| Acknowledge any correction, removal, report or complaint | 1 business day |
| Resolve a correction | 7 days |
| Hide a profile after a valid removal request from the person or their representative | 2 business days |

Staff side: [Corrections](/staff/corrections) and [Requests and reports](/staff/requests-and-reports).
