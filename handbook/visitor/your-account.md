# Your profile owner account

If staff approved your claim to a profile, they may have given you a Profile owner account. You use it to keep your profile up to date. It lives on the public site, at `/account/`. You never need the staff admin area.

## Getting your account

1. There is no public sign-up. Staff create the account when they approve your claim.
2. You receive an email with a "Set your password" link. Open it and choose a password.
3. If you own more than one profile, they all appear under the same account.

## Signing in

1. Scroll to the footer of any page and choose **Profile owner sign in**. When you are signed in, the same link reads **My account**.
2. Enter your email address and password.
3. If you get the details wrong too many times, sign-in is locked for a while. The page may also show a short check that you are not a robot.
4. An error never says whether an email address has an account. This protects owners' privacy.

Two-factor sign-in is optional for owners.

## Forgotten password

1. On the sign-in page, choose the lost password link.
2. Enter your email address. If it belongs to an owner account, you receive a link to set a new password.

## Your account pages

| Page | What it does |
|---|---|
| My profiles | Lists every profile you own, with its status: **Live**, or **Changes waiting for review** |
| Edit profile | Change your profile's descriptive text, links, contact details and photograph |
| Account details | Change your name and password |
| Sign out | Ends your session |

## Editing and review

1. Open a profile from **My profiles** and choose **Edit profile**.
2. Make your changes and save.
3. Your change goes to staff for review. The live profile does not change yet, and My profiles shows **Changes waiting for review**.
4. A reviewer approves the change or returns it with a note. You are emailed either way.
5. Once approved, the change is public.

## What you cannot change

- The profile's status, its web address, the Claimed mark or any label, such as Verified or Featured.
- Sponsored story arrangements.
- Your email address. To change it, contact the team and they will update it for you.

To fix something you cannot edit, use the [correction form](/visitor/corrections-and-removals).

## Signing out

Choose **Sign out** on any account page. Sign out on a shared computer when you finish.

## Privacy

Your account pages are private. Search engines do not index them, they are not in the sitemap and they are never cached. Your account details are personal data, covered by the site's data export and erasure process.
