# Get listed

The Get listed page and the Contact page are how a person or organisation reaches the team about being listed or working with WHOISWHO. Both create an item for staff. Nothing is paid on the site and nothing is published because someone sent an enquiry.

## Send a Get listed enquiry

1. Choose "Get listed" in the header.
2. Fill in the form.
3. Send it.
4. The visitor sees "Enquiry received" and gets an email with a reference such as `ENQ-0004`.
5. Staff contact the visitor. Terms are agreed offline.

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Your name | Required |
| Organisation | Optional |
| Interested in | Listing a profile, Sponsored story, Media or editorial work, Awards sponsorship, or Not sure |
| Email or phone | Required |
| Anything else | Free text |
| Consent | A tick that the visitor has read the privacy notice and agrees to be contacted about this enquiry |

There are no packages, tiers, cart or checkout. Every listing has the same format, and no public page states prices.

## If a listing is agreed

Staff either create the profile on the customer's behalf or email the customer a single-use invitation link.

| Link | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Invitation link | The customer fills in their own profile details. No account or password. A reviewer checks everything before it is public. |
| Consent link | A person agrees to a complimentary listing. Consent is recorded with a timestamp, and the profile is not public until they agree and a reviewer approves it. |

Each link works once, expires, is tied to one purpose and carries no personal data in the address. An expired or used link shows "Link expired or already used" with a button to ask the team for a new one.

## Send a general message

1. Open Contact from the footer.
2. Enter a name, an email address and a message, and complete the bot check.
3. The visitor sees "Message sent". A member of staff replies.

The Contact page also points to the quickest routes for common needs: the correction form on a profile, Get listed, complaints and takedown, and data rights.

## Contact the owner of a profile

Each profile has "Send an enquiry". The message goes to staff, who forward it. The owner's email and phone are never shown. After several enquiries in a short time the visitor is asked to try later.

Staff side: [Enquiries](/staff/enquiries).
