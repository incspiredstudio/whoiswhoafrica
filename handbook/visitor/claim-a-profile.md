# Claim a profile

A person, or a representative of an organisation, can ask to take care of a profile. Claiming needs no account. Staff check the evidence and decide within five days.

## Claim a profile

1. Open the profile and choose "Claim this profile".
2. Fill in the form: whether you are the person or an authorised representative, your name, your role, your work email, a phone number (optional), how staff can check who you are, and a consent tick.
3. Send it. The visitor sees a confirmation with a reference and receives an acknowledgement email.
4. Staff check the evidence offline and approve or reject the claim within five days.
5. The visitor is emailed the decision. A rejection includes a reason.

## What changes after approval

| Where | What changes |
|---|---|
| Staff screens | The profile gets a Claimed checkmark. |
| Public profile | The Claim button disappears. Nothing else changes. The public never sees a Claimed or Unclaimed label. |
| The claim address | Anyone who opens it sees "Already claimed", with a link to suggest a correction. |

Claimed is not a fact-check, a trust score or a paid status.

## Keeping a claimed profile up to date

1. Staff may create a staff sign-in for the claimant with a limited role, and assign the profile to them.
2. The claimant signs in (two-factor sign-in is recommended) and sees only their assigned profiles.
3. They edit descriptive text, links, contact details and the photograph. They cannot change status, address, the Claimed mark or any label.
4. Saving sends the change for review. The live profile does not change.
5. A Reviewer or Administrator, never the author, approves it or returns it with a note. The claimant is emailed either way.

## Limits

- A profile that is waiting for review cannot be claimed until it has been reviewed.
- Anyone who cannot or does not want to claim a profile can still use the [correction form](/visitor/corrections-and-removals).
