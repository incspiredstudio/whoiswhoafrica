# Profile sections: Bio, Achievement, Influence and Legacy

Every profile page tells its story in up to four sections, named after the brand words. This page explains what belongs in each, the writing rules, and how to edit them.

## What goes where

| Section | What it answers | Example of the kind of sentence |
|---|---|---|
| **Bio** | Who they are: role, field, where they work, a short career line | "Engineer and founder of a solar company based in Accra." |
| **Achievement** | What they have done that can be checked: firsts, results, awards, milestones | "Led the team that connected 40,000 homes to off-grid power by 2024." |
| **Influence** | How their work changed others: people trained, policies shaped, industries moved | "Her training programme has been adopted by three national utilities." |
| **Legacy** | What lasts: institutions built, work that continues, for people who have died how they are remembered | "The foundation he set up still funds 200 scholarships a year." |

All types of profile (individual, entrepreneur, organisation, institution) can have all four. A section left empty does not appear on the page. Organisations also keep their short summary.

## Writing rules

The same five review points apply as for every fact on the site:

1. No allegations, litigation or rumours.
2. No opinion or praise ("visionary", "the greatest").
3. No personal life, family, health or finances.
4. Every claim can be traced to a source on the profile.
5. Professional facts only.

Practical limits:

- Plain text only. No links, bold or headings inside the text.
- Up to 6,000 characters per section. Most sections are 2 to 5 sentences.
- Leave a blank line between paragraphs. The page shows them as separate paragraphs.
- Write in plain English, in the third person and in the past tense for finished events.

Some imported sections were drafted with AI by the acquisition team and are marked with the method "ai" in the review screen. Read these closely against the sources before approving.

## Edit the sections

1. Open **Directory > Profiles**, find the profile and open it.
2. Choose **Edit profile**. The four sections are text boxes under **Edit fields**.
3. Change the text.
4. Fill in **Reason or request reference (required)**, for example "Added 2025 award to Achievement" or the reference of a correction request.
5. Press **Save changes**. The message "Saved. A version and an audit row were written." confirms it.

If the profile is published, the public page changes at once. Every save keeps the previous version, so a change can be traced and undone.

If the profile is claimed and you are its assigned profile manager, your change waits for a Reviewer or Administrator before it shows. See [Claims](/staff/claims#the-assigned-user).

## On the public page

The profile page shows the four sections in order, each with its heading, with comfortable line lengths and paragraph breaks. For people who have died, the lifespan appears under the name.
