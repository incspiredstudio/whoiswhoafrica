# Corrections

Suggested corrections from the public land on the Corrections screen. Staff apply the change, mark it resolved or decline it with a reason. The public promise is acknowledgement within one business day and resolution within seven days.

## Who does what

| Work | Capability | Default roles |
|---|---|---|
| Open the Corrections screen | `wiw_support_inbox` | Editor, Administrator |
| Decide corrections | `wiw_handle_disputes` | Editor, Administrator |

An unresolved dispute goes to the Administrator. The Administrator can grant the deciding capability to others.

## Work a correction

1. Open **Review > Corrections**. The list shows reference, profile, the suggestion, received date, due date and status, and filters by status.
2. Open an item. You see everything the requester sent: who they say they are, their evidence and their contact details. These are staff-only.
3. Check that the requester's email is confirmed. Until the requester opens the single-use link, the item shows "email not confirmed".
4. Choose one:
   - **Apply** opens the profile for a direct edit with a required reason. The request reference is entered as the reason. Do not edit a separate copy.
   - **Mark resolved** closes the item when no edit is needed.
   - **Decline with a reason.** The reason is kept with the request and is never public.
5. If the profile is claimed, an edit by its assigned user waits for a Reviewer or Administrator. The screen shows a hint when this applies.
6. The edit, the closure or the decline is written to the audit log, and the requester is emailed when the request is resolved.

## Time limits

| Target | Time |
|---|---|
| Acknowledge | 1 business day |
| Resolve | 7 days |

A breach of either is flagged in the Due column.

## Other requests that look similar

A request to hide a profile, a report of a problem or a data request belongs on [Requests and reports](/staff/requests-and-reports).
