# Newsletter

Visitors can sign up by email to get new stories. Marketing email needs its own opt-in, and every message carries a one-click unsubscribe. This page covers how sign-ups are recorded and what staff should check.

## How a sign-up works

1. A visitor enters an email address in the form on Stories or Home and ticks the consent text.
2. The site records the address, the time, the consent and the version of the privacy notice shown.
3. A confirmation email is sent. The visitor lands on "You are subscribed".
4. The confirmation time is recorded when the visitor opens the link.
5. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link. One click on it, with no sign-in, records the unsubscribe time and stops further marketing email.

Sign-ups are rate-limited and protected by a bot check. An invalid or empty email shows an error and creates no record.

## What staff can see

Editors and Administrators can see sign-ups and who has unsubscribed, with consent dates. Subscribers are kept in their own list, apart from enquiries and customers.

1. Open **Audience > Subscribers**.
2. Choose a list at the top: **Confirmed**, **Pending** (signed up but has not opened the confirmation link) or **Unsubscribed**. Each shows its count.
3. The table shows the email, when consent was given, when it was confirmed, when they unsubscribed and where they signed up.

## Send a newsletter

The site collects and keeps the list, and it has the one-click unsubscribe built in. It has no screen for writing or sending a newsletter. Agree how the first newsletter is sent with the development team before you send it, so that unsubscribes are kept in step.

What an Administrator can do today:

1. Open **Audience > Subscribers**, choose **Confirmed** and press **Export this list as CSV**. The export is written to the audit log.
2. Use only the confirmed list, and export it again right before each send, so anyone who unsubscribed since is left out.
3. Any mail service used for sending must be on the processor register and must put an unsubscribe link in every message.
4. Delete old exports from your computer.

## Staff checks

| Check | Why |
|---|---|
| Never email an address that has unsubscribed | The unsubscribe is a legal promise |
| Send marketing email only to confirmed subscribers | Consent is recorded per address |
| Include the unsubscribe link in every message | Required in every marketing email |
| Add any new mail service to the processor register | Anything that handles personal data must be listed. See [Privacy and data rights](/concepts/privacy-and-data-rights). |

## Data rights

A subscriber appears in the data-request export for their email address, and can be erased on request.
