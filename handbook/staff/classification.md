# Classification

Every profile has a place in the directory: a **country** and an **industry** under a **sector**. That place gives the profile its breadcrumb on the public page, for example:

`Nigeria › Industry & Manufacturing › Construction & Cement › Dangote Cement`

## The parts

| Part | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Country** | One main country: the head-office country of an organisation, or the main country of a person. Pan-African bodies use **Pan-African**. |
| **City** | Optional. Only cities of the chosen country can be picked. |
| **Industry** | One main industry, chosen from the industries grouped by sector. The sector comes with it. Choose **Unclassified (needs classification)** when you cannot tell yet. |
| **Other industries** | Optional. They list the profile on those industries' pages too, but do not change its breadcrumb. |
| **Organisation type** | Organisations and institutions only: one type, for example Company, University or NGO / non-profit. |

An unclassified profile shows `Country › Name`; a profile with no country shows `Directory › Name`.

## Set or change it

1. Open the profile on **Edit profile** (see [Edit a profile](/staff/edit-profile)), or fill it in on [Add profile](/staff/add-profile).
2. In **Classification**:
   - **Industry**: type a few letters in the box above the list to narrow it, then choose the industry.
   - **Other industries**: the profile's own extras show as ticked chips. Untick one to remove it. To add one, choose it in **Add another industry**.
   - **Organisation type**, **Country** and **City**: choose from the lists. The city list follows the country.
3. Give a reason and press **Save changes**.

The change writes a version and an audit row like any other edit. Only Editors and Administrators can change it.

If the industry or country you need is missing, add it first: see [Countries, industries and organisation types](/staff/lists).

## Profiles that need classification

**Directory > Profiles** has two views at the top: **All** and **Needs classification**, each with its count. Needs classification lists every profile whose main industry is missing or Unclassified, or that has no main country. Work through it by opening each profile and setting its classification.

Imported profiles come classified by the acquisition team. A record whose industry could not be matched arrives as Unclassified and appears in this view.
