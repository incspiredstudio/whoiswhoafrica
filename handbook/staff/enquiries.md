# Enquiries

Everything a visitor sends through the Get listed form, the Contact form or a profile's enquiry form arrives on the Enquiries screen. This page explains how to work an item and what to do when a deal is agreed.

## Who does what

| Work | Capability | Default roles |
|---|---|---|
| Work enquiries | `wiw_support_inbox` | Editor, Administrator |
| Create a customer or open a sponsored story deal | `wiw_manage_register` (fixed) | Administrator |

## Where items come from

| Form | Item |
|---|---|
| Get listed (listing, sponsored story, media work, awards sponsorship) | Enquiry |
| Contact | Enquiry |
| Send an enquiry on a profile | Enquiry, relayed to the profile's owner by staff |

## Work an enquiry

1. Open Enquiries. Each item has a status and an age.
2. Open an item and mark it read.
3. Set an owner so one person is responsible.
4. Reply by email. Staff relay profile enquiries and never reveal the owner's email or phone.
5. If a listing is agreed, either create the profile on the customer's behalf or issue a single-use invitation link.
6. If a sponsored story is agreed, an Administrator creates the customer and opens a deal. See [Sponsored stories](/staff/sponsored-stories).

Enquiries carry no due date.

## Customers

A customer record holds an external ID, name, segment and contact. Administrators create customers directly, on the Customers screen or from New sponsored story. The record keeps a stable ID and change timestamps.

## Invitation and consent links

The Administrator issues these with "Issue invitation or consent link". Each works once, expires, and is tied to one purpose.

| Link | Use |
|---|---|
| Invitation | A customer fills in their own profile. A reviewer checks it before it is public. |
| Consent | A person agrees to a complimentary listing. The profile is not public until they agree and a reviewer approves it. |

A complimentary listing is left out of paid counts.

## Profile enquiries and switches

On the profile screen, staff set what contact details show publicly and whether enquiries are allowed. Owners ask for this through the correction form.

## Rules

- Paying never changes how a profile is ranked, labelled, nominated or judged.
- No public page states prices or packages.
