# Who to contact

Use this page to find who to ask. Fill in the names and contact details in the team's own contact sheet, not here: this handbook is shared, and it holds no personal details or passwords.

## Who handles what

| Problem | First contact | Then |
|---|---|---|
| How to do a task in wp-admin | This handbook, then a WHOISWHO Administrator | The development team |
| A profile, story or comment that looks wrong | An Editor | The Administrator |
| A removal request, complaint or legal letter | The Administrator on duty, the same day | The person responsible for legal matters |
| A suspected data breach (personal data seen by someone who should not have it) | The Administrator, immediately | The person responsible for data protection, who decides about telling the regulator |
| Locked out of your account | Another Administrator | The development team |
| The site is down, slow or shows an error | The Administrator | The hosting company's support, then the development team |
| Email not arriving | The Administrator ([Troubleshooting](/operations/troubleshooting#email-not-sending)) | The hosting company's support |
| Disk full, PHP version, database permissions, backups | The hosting company's support | The development team |
| A new WHOISWHO bundle, a database change waiting, a bug in WHOISWHO | The development team | |
| Data from the acquisition team: a refused bundle, a wrong record | The acquisition team | |
| Brand, photos and licences | The brand lead | |

## What to send when you ask for help

1. What you were trying to do.
2. What you saw: a screenshot, the exact message, the page address.
3. When it happened (date and time).
4. What you already tried.

Never send passwords, recovery codes or backup files by email or chat. Do not paste people's personal details into a ticket; give the request reference or the profile address instead.

## The contact sheet

Keep one sheet, owned by the Administrator, with these lines filled in:

| Role | What to record |
|---|---|
| Administrators (at least two) | Name, work email, phone |
| Development team | Name, email, hours, how to report something urgent |
| Hosting company | Support address or ticket page, the account or customer number |
| Domain and DNS provider | Where the domain is registered |
| Acquisition team | Contact for bundles and data questions |
| Data protection lead | Name and email |
| Legal contact | Name and email |
| Brand lead | Name and email |

Review the sheet whenever someone joins or leaves.
