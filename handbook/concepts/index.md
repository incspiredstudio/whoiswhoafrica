## The principles in one list

1. Facts carry sources. A fact without a source is not shown.
2. A person reviews before an individual goes public.
3. Payment never affects recognition, ranking or labels.
4. Visitors have no accounts, so there is nothing to take over.
5. Personal data is minimised, time-limited and catalogued.
6. Every staff action that matters is audited.
