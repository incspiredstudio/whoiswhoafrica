# Theme and editing

This section explains how the site's look is organised and what staff can change themselves. The public pages come from one parent theme and one child theme. Brand values live in one place, and page copy is edited in the WordPress block editor.

## Design aims

- Confident type, a strict brand palette and motion that never blocks reading.
- Layouts that work at 360 pixels wide as well as on large screens.
- Every page works without motion or scripts.
- Colour is never the only signal. Labels differ in shape as well as colour, and text meets contrast checks.
- Money never shapes the look. The Sponsored label is the only paid disclosure, and only stories carry it.

## Who changes what

| Change | Who | How |
|---|---|---|
| Words on About, FAQ, Awards rules, legal pages and Home | Editors | Page editor |
| Pending-review label wording, Nominations switch | Administrator | wp-admin |
| Colours, fonts, logo, spacing | A developer | The child theme's tokens file |
| Templates and components | A developer | The parent theme, or a copy in the child |
