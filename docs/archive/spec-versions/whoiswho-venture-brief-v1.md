# **1\. INTRODUCTION: WHAT WE ARE BUILDING**

WHOISWHO is an African discovery, recognition and business intelligence platform built to identify, document, profile, connect and celebrate the people, professionals, entrepreneurs, organisations and institutions shaping Africa.

Africa has no shortage of exceptional people, businesses, institutions and emerging leaders. However, their stories, achievements, contributions and professional identities are often scattered across social media, websites, news publications and disconnected professional networks.

There is a gap between being accomplished and being discoverable. There is also a gap between making an impact today and ensuring that the impact is remembered tomorrow.

WHOISWHO exists to close that gap.

We are building a structured platform that makes notable African people, businesses and institutions easier to discover, understand, recognise and connect with.

The platform will combine:

* Professional and business profiles.  
* Institutional and organisational listings.  
* Editorial features and media publicity.  
* Recognition and awards.  
* Industry and sector directories.  
* Professional discovery and networking.  
* Paid subscriptions and premium visibility.  
* Events and industry engagement.  
* Business and institutional intelligence.

WHOISWHO is not simply a website where people upload their profiles. It is a platform that builds visibility, credibility, discoverability and recognition around the people and organisations contributing to Africa's development.

Our ambition is to become a trusted reference point for discovering who matters, what they do, what they have achieved and the legacy they are building.

## **The central brand idea**

**INFLUENCE. ACHIEVEMENT. LEGACY.**

These three words define everything WHOISWHO represents.

* Influence is about the people and organisations making a difference and shaping the world around them.  
* Achievement is about the milestones, contributions, innovations and accomplishments that deserve recognition.  
* Legacy is about the lasting impact people and institutions leave behind.

WHOISWHO exists to document influence, celebrate achievement and preserve legacy.

# **3\. WHAT WHOISWHO STANDS FOR**

The brand will be built around five fundamental values.

### **3.1 Influence**

Influence is not limited to fame, wealth, popularity or social media following.

It includes the ability to create change, shape ideas, lead organisations, inspire people, advance industries and contribute meaningfully to society.

WHOISWHO seeks to identify and document influence across different fields and levels of society.

### **3.2 Achievement**

We believe meaningful achievements deserve to be documented.

From building successful businesses to advancing scientific knowledge, creating jobs, transforming communities, developing institutions and delivering outstanding professional work, achievements come in different forms.

WHOISWHO celebrates substance, not just publicity.

### **3.3 Legacy**

An individual's or institution's significance should not be measured only by present-day visibility.

Legacy considers what remains, what has been built, who has benefited and what will continue beyond the individual or organisation.

WHOISWHO wants to become a lasting repository of stories, achievements and institutional histories.

Recognition must mean something.

We must distinguish between verified information, editorial opinions, paid visibility and award recognition.

Payment for a subscription must never automatically translate into an award or an independently verified achievement.

### **3.5 Opportunity**

Visibility should create opportunities.

Our platform should eventually facilitate introductions, partnerships, business enquiries, networking, speaking opportunities, recruitment and other meaningful connections.

WHOISWHO needs an effective way to introduce itself to the market.

Rather than launching quietly as another directory website, we will use a flagship recognition programme to introduce the brand, generate conversations and attract our first community of subscribers.

The maiden awards edition will serve three purposes:

**1\. Brand Introduction**

It introduces WHOISWHO to business leaders, professionals, institutions, entrepreneurs, media organisations and the general public.

**2\. Community Acquisition**

It gives us a reason to approach notable people and organisations, build relationships and bring the first participants onto the platform.

**3\. Commercial Validation**

It allows us to test whether individuals and organisations are willing to pay for visibility, profiling, media exposure and participation in a credible recognition ecosystem.

The awards are our launch vehicle. The WHOISWHO platform is the business we are building.

# **9\. THE COMMERCIAL TARGET: 10 PAID SUBSCRIPTIONS**

The minimum target is deliberately achievable but commercially meaningful.

Ten paying subscribers will help us establish whether the value proposition resonates with the intended market.

However, the team should not treat 10 as an ambition ceiling.

**10 is the minimum acceptable outcome. The team should aim higher.**

## **9.1 Suggested Initial Customer Mix**

| Customer Segment | Target |
| ----- | ----- |
| Individual professionals and executives | 4 |
| Entrepreneurs and founders | 2 |
| Businesses and companies | 2 |
| Institutions and organisations | 2 |
| **Total** | **10** |

This is a suggested acquisition mix, not a restriction on who can subscribe.

## **9.2 Prospecting Requirements**

To secure 10 paid subscriptions, the team must maintain a sufficiently large pipeline.

Initial working assumptions:

* 500 qualified prospects identified.  
* 300 prospects meaningfully contacted.  
* 150 prospects engaged in conversations.  
* 100 prospects receive a proposal or subscription offer.  
* 10 prospects convert into paying subscribers.

These are planning assumptions, not guaranteed conversion rates.

The team should monitor the actual conversion rates and adjust its acquisition strategy.

## **9.3 What Counts as a Subscription?**

A subscription only counts towards the target when:

* The customer has selected an approved subscription package.  
* Payment has been received and confirmed.  
* The customer has been entered into the subscription register.  
* The customer has completed or initiated the required onboarding process.

Verbal commitments, unpaid invoices, expressions of interest and complimentary profiles do not count.

Complimentary listings may be used strategically to build directory credibility, but must be reported separately.
